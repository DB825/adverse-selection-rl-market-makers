# Entropy regularization experiment — fixed protocol

Recorded before treatment training or inspecting its outcomes, 27 September 2026. The user authorized the proposed entropy-only comparison after the GAE diagnostic failed to establish useful recurrent behavior. Earlier test cohorts have informed development; they are not fresh confirmation for this experiment.

Train exactly five recurrent models, seeds 11–15, for 300,032 transitions using `configs/entropy_001.yaml`. Change only entropy coefficient from zero to **0.01** relative to the existing `results/followup_budget/recurrent` controls. Keep GAE .95, gamma one, learning rate .0003, architecture, batch/rollout settings, simulator, observation features and economic evaluation objective unchanged. Entropy regularization changes the optimization loss; it is not additional economic profit. No schedules, coefficient sweep, seed replacement, intermediate checkpoint selection or extension based on returns.

Pair initialization weights and simulator streams within each seed. Reuse existing zero-entropy checkpoints and train only the five new models. Independent training seeds are the replication unit; the two arms within a seed are paired. All final checkpoints remain in the analysis, including abstaining or fixed-quote policies.

Reserve **1,024 development episodes starting at 10,000,000**, **2,048 final economic test episodes starting at 11,000,000**, and **1,024 conditional probe episodes starting at 12,000,000**. No development-based model selection is planned, so keep development unused unless an implementation issue requires diagnosis. Both arms use identical customer tapes and separate paired categorical-action uniforms on the final cohort. Quotes still induce different executions. Reevaluate existing feedforward, history-8 and belief-input policies on that cohort and include predeclared fixed actions, abstention and Bayesian myopic. Only the recurrent entropy comparison isolates the training factor; other policy families are contextual baselines.

The primary contrast is the paired economic objective difference (entropy .01 minus zero). Report every seed, an approximate t4 interval over five seed differences, and a separate whole-episode bootstrap interval after averaging paired differences over the fixed five fitted policies. Neither is a joint uncertainty interval. Keep exposure, tails, participation, regime breakdowns and each seed's true action probabilities visible. A higher entropy or more frequent trading is not sufficient evidence of useful adaptation.

Use the existing behavior gate: economically useful gains over predeclared fixed wide quoting supported by several seeds, together with meaningful input-dependent actions. Compare against the contextual public-observation baselines separately. If this fails, record the failure and defer substantive probes. If it passes, freeze all five treatment models and run the existing whole-episode probe panel on the separate 12,000,000 cohort, including failed seeds and same-history untrained-network controls. Maintain fixed-quote targets, train-only scaling, validation-only ridge selection, all-target reporting and separate seed/episode uncertainty. Linear accessibility is not proof of older-history information or causal use. No causal intervention or further training sweep is included.

Keep prior experiments intact. Record serialized entropy/GAE settings, source and checkpoint hashes, dependency versions, finite optimization diagnostics, and any undefined explained-variance metrics explicitly. The repository-status assessment is separate from the scientific experiment; no public repository is created by this protocol.
